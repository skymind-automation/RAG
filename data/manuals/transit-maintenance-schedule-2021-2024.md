# Transit 350 Owner Maintenance Schedule (Severe Duty / Fleet)

## Which Schedule Applies

Fleet vehicles almost always fall under the severe-duty schedule, not the
normal schedule printed in the owner's manual. Any one of the following
qualifies a vehicle as severe duty:

- Sustained operation with payload above 70% of GVWR
- More than 50% of operating time in stop-and-go delivery routes
- Extended idling (more than one hour per operating day)
- Towing
- Operation in sustained ambient temperatures above 32°C or below -12°C
- Operation on unpaved roads or in high-dust environments

Advisors should assume severe duty for any vehicle on a delivery, utility or
service-body contract unless the customer states otherwise.

## Interval Table - Severe Duty

| Interval | Service |
| --- | --- |
| Every 8,000 km / 5,000 mi | Engine oil and filter, tire rotation, multi-point inspection |
| Every 16,000 km / 10,000 mi | Cabin air filter, brake inspection, fluid level check |
| Every 48,000 km / 30,000 mi | Engine air filter, front and rear differential fluid inspection |
| Every 96,000 km / 60,000 mi | Automatic transmission fluid and filter, rear axle fluid, spark plugs (gasoline), fuel filter (diesel), accessory drive belt inspection, brake fluid |
| Every 160,000 km / 100,000 mi | Coolant (initial fill), transfer case fluid (AWD), PCV valve |
| Every 36 months | Brake fluid, regardless of mileage |

## 60,000 Mile Service - Detail

The 60,000 mi (96,000 km) service is the largest scheduled visit in the first
100,000 miles and is the one advisors are asked to quote most often. It
comprises:

1. **Engine oil and filter.** 5W-30 synthetic blend, 6.1 L with filter on the
   3.5L gasoline engine; 5W-30 full synthetic, 12.9 L on the 2.0L diesel.
2. **Automatic transmission fluid and filter.** 10R60 transmission. Fluid is
   Mercon ULV only; a universal ATF will cause shift shudder within a few
   thousand miles and is not covered by warranty.
3. **Rear axle fluid.** 75W-140 synthetic on limited-slip axles, with friction
   modifier. Omitting friction modifier on a limited-slip axle produces a
   chatter on slow turns that is frequently misdiagnosed as a tire problem.
4. **Spark plugs** (gasoline only). Gap is preset; do not re-gap iridium plugs.
5. **Fuel filter** (diesel only). Prime the system before cranking.
6. **Engine air filter and cabin air filter.**
7. **Accessory drive belt inspection.** Replace if cracking exceeds three
   cracks per 25 mm in any rib.
8. **Brake fluid replacement** if not done within 36 months.
9. **Full brake inspection**, including pad thickness and rotor thickness
   variation measurement.
10. **Road test**, including a 15% grade parking brake hold check.

Typical fleet quote assumes 4.5 labour hours. Add 0.5 hours for AWD vehicles
(transfer case fluid inspection) and 0.7 hours for dual-rear-wheel
configurations.

## 30,000 Mile Service - Detail

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Engine air filter | Replace; inspect airbox seal |
| Differential fluid inspection | Inspect only, unless contaminated |
| Brake inspection | Pad thickness, rotor condition, hose condition |
| Tire rotation | Include spare if full-size |
| Multi-point inspection | Includes battery load test |

## Fluid Capacities and Specifications

| System | Specification | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine oil (3.5L gasoline) | 5W-30 synthetic blend | 6.1 L |
| Engine oil (2.0L diesel) | 5W-30 full synthetic | 12.9 L |
| Automatic transmission (10R60) | Mercon ULV | 11.8 L total fill |
| Rear axle (limited slip) | 75W-140 synthetic + friction modifier | 2.6 L |
| Engine coolant | Orange silicate-free extended life | 14.2 L |
| Brake fluid | DOT 4 LV | 0.9 L |

Do not mix coolant colours. Mixing silicate-free extended-life coolant with a
conventional green coolant produces a gel that blocks the heater core, and the
first symptom the customer reports is no heat, not overheating.

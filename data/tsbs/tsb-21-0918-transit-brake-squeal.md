# TSB 21-0918 - Front Brake Squeal at Low Speed

## Applicability

2021-2022 Transit 350, all wheelbases, built before 2022-06-20.

## Symptom

A high-pitched squeal from the front brakes during light braking below
30 km/h (20 mph), most pronounced in the first few stops of the day and in
damp weather. Braking performance is unaffected. Pad thickness is within
specification and the wear indicator is not in contact.

Customers describe this as "squealing when I brake", "a squeak at low speed"
or "noise in the morning that goes away". It is frequently mistaken for worn
pads and quoted as a pad replacement when the pads are nearly new.

## Cause

The original anti-rattle clip design allows the outboard pad to rock slightly
in the anchor bracket during low-pressure braking. The resulting high-frequency
vibration is audible as squeal. The condition is more pronounced when surface
moisture raises the friction coefficient on the first stops of the day.

## Correction

Do not machine or replace the rotors for this condition, and do not replace
pads that are within specification.

1. Verify pad thickness is above the 3.0 mm service limit and the wear
   indicator is not contacting.
2. Verify rotor thickness variation is within 0.015 mm. If it is not, this
   bulletin does not apply — address the rotor condition.
3. Remove the front pads.
4. Install revised anti-rattle clip kit, part number BRK-2211-K (supersedes
   BRK-1908-K).
5. Apply high-temperature caliper grease to the pad backing plate contact
   points only. Do not grease the friction surface or the clip face.
6. Verify caliper slide pins move freely; replace boots if torn.
7. Torque caliper guide pin bolts to 36 N·m (27 lb-ft).
8. Bed the pads: ten moderate stops from 60 km/h to 20 km/h with cooling
   intervals.

## Advisor Notes

Worth checking on any Transit in this range that comes in with a brake noise
complaint, before quoting a brake job. A clip kit at 0.8 hours is a very
different conversation from a front brake service, and quoting the brake job
first and finding the clip kit second is how fleet customers lose confidence in
an estimate.
